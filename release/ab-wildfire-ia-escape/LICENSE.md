# Licensing

Three grants with three scopes. Nothing here relicenses anyone else's data.

## Our code and documentation: MIT

See `LICENSE-MIT.txt`. Covers the construction, qualification, references
and helper scripts and this documentation. The `baseline/` runner files
(train.py, validate.py, validate.sh) are the harness benchmark's own MIT
files, shipped verbatim and unmodified at the sha256 recorded in
`measurements.json`.

## Our rights in the derived compilation: CC0-1.0

See `LICENSE-CC0-1.0.txt`. The dedication covers **our contribution only**:
the all-cause assessment-instant population frame, the derived binary escape
label, the at-instant feature derivations (elapsed hours, calendar fields),
the enforced column policy, the winter-carryover split rule and the temporal
partition. It does not cover the source records.

## The source's own terms, preserved

One preserved upstream instrument, not relicensed. The Government of Alberta's Historical wildfire data 2006-2025 (per-fire incident records, Forest Protection Area) is licensed under the Open Government Licence - Alberta v2.2, with its attribution requirement, non-endorsement clause, no-warranty terms and (where applicable) personal-information exemption. The redistributed fields are the publisher's own assessment-instant record (assessment size, rate of spread, fire behaviour type, position on slope, in-situ weather over the fire, FBP fuel type, detection channel), pre-instant timing fields and location, plus our derived label, elapsed-time features and partition. The derived dataset is labelled as such throughout and is neither the Government of Alberta's official product nor endorsed by it.
