# euipo-opposition-outcome

Record dated evidence, decisions, and failed approaches here.

## 2026-11-03 discovery session (EU/EEA regional focus)

Considered at shortlisting: predict EUIPO Opposition Division outcomes (Art. 8(1)
EUTMR likelihood of confusion) from the two marks' text/images and Nice classes, at
opposition filing. Killed at the prior-art sweep, BEFORE any EUIPO probing or licence
reading - the released, scored prior art settles it as a mirror:

- Haller 2023 (TU Wien, "TrademarkML: predicting the likelihood of confusion"):
  TMSIM-500, 500 EUIPO opposition cases, released at doi 10.7910/DVN/PNFQLC,
  published ML pipeline + train/test splits (cases kept together).
- arXiv 2201.01164 ("Interpretable Low-Resource Legal Decision Making"): released
  trademark case-law dataset - 525 lawyer-annotated CJEU cases + ~2,852 weakly
  labelled EUIPO Opposition/BoA decisions from a ~52,000-case pool; RoBERTa scored.
- ICAIL 2023 (ULiege): 8,126 EUIPO Opposition Division decisions (2016-2022)
  assembled as evaluation data.
- Simmons & Simpson "Rocketeer" (World Trademark Review 2023): commercial predictor
  of EU trademark conflict outcomes from ~7,400 opposition decisions, ~93% reported.

Search used (2026-11-03): "EUIPO opposition decision prediction dataset machine
learning trademark". Licence intentionally not triaged: no permission of any kind is
recorded here, and the record must not be read as licence evidence for EUIPO data.
