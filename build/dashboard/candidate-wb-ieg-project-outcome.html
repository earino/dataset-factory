The question

what a model is asked to predict

For a World Bank (IBRD/IDA) lending project at the moment of Board approval, will the Independent Evaluation Group's final evaluation of the project rate its outcome Moderately Satisfactory or better (MS+) on the six-point IEG outcome scale? This is an approval-instant portfolio-risk task over IEG's rated-project universe (12,629 rated projects, approval FY 1956-2024, measured 2026-10-22).

What you predict

target definition

Binary from the IEG outcome rating (six-point scale: Highly Satisfactory / Satisfactory / Moderately Satisfactory / Moderately Unsatisfactory / Unsatisfactory / Highly Unsatisfactory): positive = MS+ (top three levels), negative = MU or worse. Measured mix: Satisfactory 5,639; Moderately Satisfactory 3,153; Unsatisfactory 1,870; Moderately Unsatisfactory 1,224; Highly Satisfactory 398; Highly Unsatisfactory 194; plus 151 rows 'Not Rated' which are unobserved targets, never negative labels. The label source column is outcome; the correlated IEG rating columns (bank_performance, quality_at_entry, quality_of_supervision, me_quality) are label-side and must be absent from all shipped files, not merely unlisted.

When the prediction is made

the information cut-off

Board approval of the project (approval_fy / boardapprovaldate). Features may use only attributes documented by the publisher as existing at approval: approval fiscal year and Board month, agreement type, lending instrument, product line, sector and theme tags, country, region, commitment amounts, project name. Every IEG-recorded judgement is post-hoc and label-side: outcome (the label source), bank_performance, quality_at_entry, quality_of_supervision, me_quality, evaluation_fy, evaluation_type, final_closing_fy, as_of_date. Two more columns are measured to be evaluation-vintage, NOT approval-vintage, and must not ship as features: country_fcs_status and country_lending_group - 165 of 4,695 (country, approval_fy) cells carry more than one distinct FCS value across rows, and the value flips with evaluation_fy (e.g. Cameroon approval-FY-1995: P038801 evaluated 1997 -> non-FCS, P000216 evaluated 2008 -> FCS); 51 of 187 countries carry >1 FCS value across their rows. The label arrives at IEG evaluation: median 8 years after approval (min 1, max 31, measured over 12,629 rows).
