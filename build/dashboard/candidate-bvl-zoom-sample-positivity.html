The question

what a model is asked to predict

For a food sample drawn under the German zoonoses monitoring programme (Zoonosen-Monitoring, ZooM) from a stated matrix, will it test positive for Campylobacter spp., given only the sampling-plan and item attributes decided when the sample is taken: reporting year, sampling date/month, matrix and matrix group, sampling stage (slaughterhouse/retail etc.), production type (conventional/organic), origin country, processing (chilled/frozen...), packaging, and programme?

What you predict

target definition

Binary from the source row: ErgAlphaT = 'positiv; nachgewiesen' (detected) vs 'negativ; nicht nachgewiesen' (not detected), restricted to rows whose Parameter is the Campylobacter genus detection ('Genus Campylobacter'; Parameter/ParameterT1 columns). Label source columns ErgAlpha, ErgAlphaT and the legally derived assessment Bewertung/BewertungT ('Beanstandet'/'Ohne Beanstandung') are recorded in label_source_columns and must be absent from the shipped features. Unit/frame: qualitative presence/absence per sample at the method's declared analytical amount (Methode/Probenvorbereitung columns, e.g. 10 g), the publisher's own frame - no external threshold is compared, so no known-event fixture is required. Rows where a quantitative result or a different parameter vocabulary appears are out of frame and must be pinned at construction from the Datensatzbeschreibung column dictionary.

When the prediction is made

the information cut-off

The moment the sample is taken: every feature is an attribute of the sampling plan or of the sampled item as recorded at sampling (Jahr, PNDat, MatrixGr/Matrix, Betriebsart, ZusA, HerkStaat, Verarbeitung, Verpackung, PNMG); the label is the laboratory result reported afterwards (ErgAlphaT 'positiv; nachgewiesen' / 'negativ; nicht nachgewiesen' from the programme's standardised qualitative detection methods). No post-hoc field is needed as a feature. A secondary parameter family with the same timing (ESBL/AmpC-bildende E. coli) is measured as a fallback in case Campylobacter proves too thin at construction.
