ready · ZA 09:50 UTC

The question

what a model is asked to predict

Primary (onset) formulation: for a South African municipality whose most recently RELEASED audit opinion (financial year ending 30 June, year_end <= k-1) was clean-ish (unqualified, with or without emphasis of matter), will its financial year k audit outcome be qualified-or-worse? Secondary (level) formulation, only reported beside the persistence baseline: will any municipality receive a qualified-or-worse opinion for FY k? Both are stated at the statutory annual-financial-statements submission instant.

What you predict

target definition

Binary label from the audit_opinions cube ('Code' column, bulk CSV audit_opinion_facts on munimoney-media S3): 1 = qualified, disclaimer or adverse; 0 = unqualified or unqualified_emphasis_of_matter; 'outstanding' rows (audit not completed) are excluded, not imputed. Label source column is never shipped as a feature: the label CSV is used only to build y; features come from the separate incexp/cflow/capital/bsheet/grants/uifwexp/repmaint/aged-debtor/aged-creditor cubes and the demographics API. Measured per-year positive rates (qualified-or-worse, outstanding excluded): 35.0%-56.5% across all 14 label years, never degenerate. Measured persistence: 83.88% year-over-year agreement (2018-2020), so the level label is persistence-shaped and the primary task is the deterioration-onset conditioning; onset base rate measured 69/381 = 18.1% of clean-prev municipality-years in 2018-2020.

When the prediction is made

the information cut-off

31 August of year k: the statutory deadline for a municipality to submit its FY k annual financial statements to the Auditor-General (AFS due three months after the 30 June year end; exact MFMA section wording to be verified at construction). At that instant the FY k audit has not been performed, so the label cannot exist. Knowable features: FY k's as-submitted (pre-audit) figures, budget phases already tabled (original/tabled budgets for FY k and FY k+1), audited actuals and audit opinions for strictly earlier years (observed general-report tabling dates: 27 Aug 2024 for FY2022-23 and 24 Jun 2026 for FY2024-25, i.e. 12-14 months after year end, so opinions are conservatively treated as known only through year_end k-2), plus static municipality demographics. Everything post-instant (FY k audit results, later tabled budgets, IBY/ITY forecast vintages) is excluded.
