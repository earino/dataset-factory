The question

what a model is asked to predict

For a complaint submitted to the CFPB Consumer Complaint Database, will the company close it with monetary or non-monetary relief rather than explanation alone?

What you predict

target definition

Binary from the documented company_response_to_consumer field: positive if the response is 'Closed with monetary relief' or 'Closed with non-monetary relief', negative for 'Closed with explanation' and administrative closures. timely_response is a separate documented outcome, not used.

When the prediction is made

the information cut-off

At complaint intake: the consumer-submitted narrative plus product, issue, sub-issue, company, state, ZIP, submitted-via channel and date are known; the company response (the label) is filed later by the company in its CFPB portal response.
