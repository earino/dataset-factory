The question

what a model is asked to predict

For a Department of Irrigation and Drainage (JPS) telemetric river-level station in Malaysia, will the station's water level exceed its official published Danger threshold at any hourly reading within the next 24 hours, given the station's own water-level and rainfall history through hour H?

What you predict

target definition

Binary: any hourly water-level reading for the station in (H, H+24] strictly above the station's published Danger Level (m). The thresholds are official per-station values shown on the publisher's per-state river pages (Normal/Alert/Warning/Danger columns, measured present in the fetched HTML), not chosen by us. Label source = the future level series; ships only as the binary label.

When the prediction is made

the information cut-off

Hour H of day D: hourly telemetry through H is knowable, and no reading after H exists. Features are the station's own trailing level/rainfall statistics; every reading in (H, H+24] is label source and never ships as a feature.
