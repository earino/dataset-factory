The question

what a model is asked to predict

For an NHS England provider operating a consultant-led (Type 1) A&E department, will the percentage of its Type 1 attendances admitted, transferred or discharged within 4 hours of arrival decline in month M+1 relative to month M, given that provider's monthly A&E series (attendances by department type and booked-appointment status, over-4-hour counts, waits from decision-to-admit, emergency admissions) for all months through M, plus calendar month? A relative month-over-month change is used instead of a fixed standard threshold because the fixed thresholds measured near-uniform positive rates in recent years (see measurements).

What you predict

target definition

Binary, computed from the source row of month M+1: within4h_rate(M+1) < within4h_rate(M), where within4h_rate(Y) = 1 - Attendances over 4hrs Type 1 / A&E attendances Type 1 for the provider in month Y. Unit: proportion of Type 1 attendances within 4 hours. Label source columns: month M+1's 'A&E attendances Type 1' and 'Attendances over 4hrs Type 1' (recorded in label_source_columns; excluded from shipped columns). Volume gate: provider-month excluded unless Type 1 attendances >= 100 in both M and M+1 (measured: leaves 124 providers with >= 24 such months).

When the prediction is made

the information cut-off

End of month M, when month M's monthly CSV is published (measured: the September 2026 monthly CSV was already downloadable on 2026-10-21, i.e. a ~3 week publication lag). Every feature is derived from provider months <= M; the label is month M+1's within-4-hour rate versus month M's, and month M+1's row is published about a month after the prediction is made.
