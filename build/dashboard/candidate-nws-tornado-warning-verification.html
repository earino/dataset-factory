nws-tornado-warning-verification

investigating · US 09:27 UTC

The question

what a model is asked to predict

For an NWS tornado warning at the moment it is issued, will it be verified - will at least one tornado report fall inside its storm-based polygon during its valid time?

What you predict

target definition

Binary: the per-warning `verify` boolean returned by IEM cow for phenomena=TO, significance=W - whether >=1 tornado local storm report (buffered by cow's default 15 km) falls inside the polygon during the valid window. The method is documented on the cow page ('NWS Storm Based Warning Verification') and matches the official storm-based verification concept in NWS Instruction 10-1601 ('FAR is the number of false alarms (unverified warnings) divided by the total number of warnings'; storm report points verify warning areas).

When the prediction is made

the information cut-off

The warning's issue timestamp. Everything the warning product itself carries at issuance is a feature: issuing WFO, forecaster of record, issue/expiry times (hence time-of-day and day-of-season), the warned county list, the storm-based polygon geometry (area, perimeter, polygon-vs-county size reduction), the tornado-possible flag on parent SVR warnings, the hail/wind tags, and - via the per-warning product-text URL - the full issued text (tornado tag, damage-threat tag, storm motion). The label is determined afterwards by which tornado reports occur in the polygon while the warning is live; the cow fields that encode it (verify, stormreports, stormreports_all, lead0, areaverify, statuses, status) are label-source/post-hoc and never ship as features.
