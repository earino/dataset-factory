The question

what a model is asked to predict

Two formulations were measured and NOT taken forward: (a) station-level next-day rainfall exceedance at an NEA rain station from its own 5-minute reading history (7,044,646 rows for 2021 alone, ~70 stations, anonymous API) - this duplicates the own-history environmental-exceedance family already carried by cpcb-aqi-exceedance (GO) and noaa-tide-flooding (live); (b) multi-disease weekly outbreak exceedance from the MOH Weekly Infectious Disease Bulletin (20,070 rows, epi_week x disease x cases since 2012-W01) - only ~29 disease entities, the exceedance threshold would be ours rather than a published band edge, and the dengue-forecasting family has the DrivenData DengAI ML-ready precedent (city-level weekly case counts, not exceedance). HDB resale price prediction - the catalog's most-mirrored dataset - exists on Kaggle. PSI exceedance duplicates (a)'s family.

What you predict

target definition

(a) daily station rainfall total on D+1 above a threshold - the threshold would be ours; Singapore's NEA publishes rainfall but no exceedance band edge for daily totals, so the label would not carry a published threshold; (b) disease-week cases above a threshold - again ours, not the publisher's. Both are mechanically derivable from the series; neither carries a publisher-defined decision edge.

When the prediction is made

the information cut-off

Stated for the not-taken-forward formulations only: (a) 23:59 SGT on day D, all readings through D knowable, D+1 readings are label source; (b) end of epi-week W, bulletin cases published after the week closes.
