published · US 09:51 UTC

The question

what a model is asked to predict

Given everything documented about a mine and its own prior inspections and citations before an inspection begins, which of the declared set of 30 CFR sections will MSHA cite at this inspection?

What you predict

target definition

The set of 30 CFR sections cited at one inspection (EVENT_NO), i.e. the distinct PART_SECTION values of its violation rows - multi-label, mean 5.79 sections per inspection, median 2. The raw label space is 5,934 sections; construction declares a floor (104 sections at >= 1% of inspections, 183 at >= 0.5%). PART_SECTION itself and every violation-level attribute of the cited rows are excluded from the shipped features.

When the prediction is made

the information cut-off

The instant the inspection begins (INSPECTION_BEGIN_DT). Every feature is either a static mine attribute (Mines table), the inspection's own trigger recorded when it opens (ACTIVITY_CODE/ACTIVITY, PROGRAM_AREA, INSPECT_OFFICE_CD), or a field of an earlier inspection of the same MINE_ID. Everything the inspector observes or records during the visit is post-hoc and excluded.
