The question

what a model is asked to predict

For an urban waste water treatment plant active in UWWTD reporting cycle N, will that plant be reported as FAILING at least one treatment-performance parameter (any of uwwBOD5Perf, uwwCODPerf, uwwNTotPerf, uwwPTotPerf, uwwTSSPerf, uwwOtherPerf = 'F') in the next reporting cycle, given only features documented and reported in cycles up to and including N: country/NUTS, organic design capacity (uwwCapacity, p.e.), load entering the plant (uwwLoadEnteringUWWTP), mean annual volume treated (uwwWasteWaterTreated), treatment train flags (primary/secondary/more-stringent/N-removal/P-removal/microfiltration/UV/ozonation...), plant age from uwwBeginLife, and the plant's own performance flags reported in cycles <= N?

What you predict

target definition

Binary from the source row of cycle N+1: label = 1 iff the plant (uwwState=1, active) has at least one treatment-performance parameter assessed 'F' among the six uww*Perf fields, restricted to plants with at least one answered (P or F) parameter; plants with only NA/NR/blank assessments are unobserved-target rows, counted separately, never dropped silently. Measured on the full 2023 call CSV: 2,411 positives / 21,537 answered (11.2% base rate). The uww*Perf codes are P/F/NA/NR (documented in the Eionet data dictionary element pages, e.g. element 104947 'Treatment performance: BOD5'); the 'In cause of failure' fields (uwwBadPerformance 'Bad performance' 1,765 Yes among active, uwwBadDesign 'Bad design or dimensioning', uwwAccidents 'Major accidents', uwwInformation free text) describe the reported CAUSE where the plant does not meet requirements and are label-source/label-adjacent columns, excluded from features. IMPORTANT measured property recorded as part of the definition: the label is 'REPORTED as failing' - a mixture of physical performance and the national reporting regime; measured 2023-call any-F rates range from DE 0.4% (16/3,776) and BE 2.8% to NO 49.6% (116/234), HR 53.5% (46/86) and RO 36.3% (352/971). Country is therefore a legitimate feature AND the reference baseline must be country-stratified; the mixture is the task's documented semantics, not a defect to hide.

When the prediction is made

the information cut-off

The publication instant of reporting cycle N (a statistical release): all feature cycles are <= N and the target is the performance assessment published in cycle N+1. Measured anchors: the 13th data call (situation at 2022-12-31 per ReportPeriod.csv) was published as FINAL PUBLIC VERSION in Oct 2025 per the catalogue record; the per-country delivery versions (repVersion) for that call are dated 2023-12 to 2025-04 (e.g. SI 2025-04-24, most MS 2024-06). Cycle cadence is biennial (Directive 91/271/EEC Art. 15, reported on the Commission's request), so the lead time is roughly two reporting cycles. The exact publication dates of the older cycles are to be pinned at construction from their catalogue records; the per-country repSituationAt/repVersion fields are already documented in the source (ReportPeriod.csv) and ship with the data.
