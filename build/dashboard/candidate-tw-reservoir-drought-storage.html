The question

what a model is asked to predict

Will a major Taiwan reservoir's effective storage ratio fall below a drought threshold (e.g. 20-30%) within the next 30 days, conditioned on it being above the threshold now (onset formulation)?

What you predict

target definition

Binary onset: storage percentage (蓄水百分比) of reservoir R crosses below the threshold on some day within (t, t+30] while above it at t. Label source column is the same series' future values; excluded from features by the window construction.

When the prediction is made

the information cut-off

Daily at the 07:00 observation instant (the dataset states 水位、有效蓄水量及蓄水百分比 are the current day's 07:00 values), using only snapshots up to that instant.
