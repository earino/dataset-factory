Hypothetical task this source would support (parked, not constructed): for an English local authority district, will its claimant count (stock of Universal Credit conditionality + JSA claimants, ONS/DWP administrative series) rise in month M+1 relative to month M, given the authority's monthly series through month M? This is the best-cross-section task found across ONS's published outputs this session; ONS publishes aggregate statistics, so any ONS-hosted task is a direction-forecast on own history plus small-area attributes.
