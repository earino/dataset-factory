The question

what a model is asked to predict

For a chosen NOAA water-level station, will the daily maximum observed water level exceed its published minor flood threshold tomorrow?

What you predict

target definition

Binary: the daily maximum observed water level for the target day exceeds the station's NOS minor flood threshold from the CO-OPS metadata API, compared at datum=STND. The physical nos_minor threshold is retained by decision; it is not replaced with a per-station quantile merely to balance the classes (see measurements.target_threshold_decision).

When the prediction is made

the information cut-off

End of the local day before the target day. Features are observed water levels only. NOAA tide predictions and model guidance are model output and must be excluded so the task stays an observation-only forecast.
