The question

what a model is asked to predict

For a dispatched Austin police 911 call, will a patrol unit reach the caller more than a fixed minutes threshold after the call is dispatched?

What you predict

target definition

Binary: portal column response_time (integer seconds) exceeds threshold T. T is frozen before any row is scored: 1200 s (20 minutes), chosen by binary search on the training window only so the training positive rate sits just at or above 0.40 - measured 0.403763 on the built training split against 0.3938 over the full series. Implemented as `late = 1 if response_time > 1200 else 0` in the construction script and recorded in quality.json as threshold_seconds.

When the prediction is made

the information cut-off

At dispatch: the moment the 911 call-taker's ECT screen opens (response_datetime). Only fields known then may be features. Arrival, closing, priority, mental-health, final-classification and injury fields are excluded.
