investigating · CA 09:27 UTC

The question

what a model is asked to predict

For a facility that reported pollutant releases to Canada's National Pollutant Release Inventory for reporting year Y, will it report nothing at all to the NPRI for year Y+1 (exit the reporting population), given only its reporting history through year Y?

What you predict

target definition

Binary and mechanical, from the publisher's own per-year products: positive iff NPRI_ID is present in the year-Y single-year data file and absent from the year-Y+1 file. NPRI_ID is the publisher's permanent facility identifier (measured: facility NPRI ID 1 carries the same ID across 2021-2024 files). Absence means the facility reported no releases/disposals/transfers for that reporting year - the documented outcome of closure, falling below reporting thresholds, or ceasing to meet criteria; the task does not need to distinguish those causes because the label is the publisher's own reporting record, not a judgement.

When the prediction is made

the information cut-off

June 1 of year Y+1: the statutory deadline for year-Y NPRI reports (NPRI reports for a calendar year are due by June 1 of the following year, per the publisher's Canada Gazette NPRI notices and reporting guides). At that instant the facility's full history through year Y is knowable, and its year-Y+1 outcome is not - the Y+1 report would not even be due until June 1 of Y+2, and the publisher publishes each reporting year's data later still (the 2024 single-year file is the latest published as of 2026-10-19). Features are strictly year-Y and earlier; every year-Y+1 field is the label source and never ships.
