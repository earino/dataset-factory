The question

what a model is asked to predict

For a CPCB continuous ambient air-quality monitoring station in India, will the station's next-day (D+1) 24-hour mean PM2.5 concentration exceed 90 micrograms/m3 - the lower breakpoint of the CPCB National AQI 'Poor' band for 24-hour PM2.5 - given only that station's own hourly PM2.5 history through the end of day D?

What you predict

target definition

Binary and mechanical from the source series: mean of the station's hourly PM2.5 values on D+1 > 90 micrograms/m3. The label source is the D+1 PM2.5 series itself, excluded from all shipped columns; the runner ships only the binary label. The threshold is a published, documented band edge (CPCB National AQI breakpoints), not a human judgement.

When the prediction is made

the information cut-off

23:59 IST on day D: every hourly reading through day D is knowable at that instant and no D+1 reading exists yet. Features are the station's own trailing hourly/daily PM2.5 statistics and calendar terms only; every D+1 measurement is label source and never ships as a feature.
