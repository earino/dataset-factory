The question

what a model is asked to predict

For a reported wildlife strike to a civil aircraft, will the strike result in damage to the aircraft?

What you predict

target definition

Binary: aircraft damage (damage level in {M, M?, S, D} on the published scale, or any damage flag) recorded in the strike report.

When the prediction is made

the information cut-off

The circumstances of the strike as recorded on the FAA strike report (form 5200-7): airport, state, FAA region, aircraft make/mass, phase of flight, height, speed, visibility, precipitation, species. Damage fields and per-part damage flags are the outcome and are excluded from features.
