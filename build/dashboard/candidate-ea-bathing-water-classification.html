The question

what a model is asked to predict

For a designated bathing water in England, given its classification history and prior-season sample-derived history through the end of season Y-1, will its annual rBWD classification for season Y (published the following autumn) be below 'Good' (Sufficient or Poor)?

What you predict

target definition

Binary from the publisher's own annual classification observation (compliance-rBWD year slice, result.primaryTopic.observation[].complianceClassification): positive if the season-Y classification is 'Sufficient' or 'Poor' (below Good); negative if 'Excellent' or 'Good'; 'Closed' waters (1-2 per season, measured) excluded. The classification is produced by the Environment Agency under the Bathing Water Regulations 2013 / revised Bathing Water Directive 2006/7/EC percentile methodology - a documented publisher process, not a human judgement applied after the fact.

When the prediction is made

the information cut-off

End of season Y-1 (30 September). Features are knowable at that instant: the water's classifications for prior seasons, its designation metadata (coastal/inland/estuarine type, district, catchment, designation year via the bathing-water-profile dataset), and prior-season sample summaries from the in-season sample slices. The label depends on season Y's own samples and the 4-season rBWD window, which do not exist at the prediction instant - the label is therefore not computable from the shipped features.
