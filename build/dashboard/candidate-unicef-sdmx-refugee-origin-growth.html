The question

what a model is asked to predict

Hypothetical task this source would support (parked on split capacity, not formulated further): for a country of origin with at least 1,000 refugees and other people in need of international protection abroad at end of year D, will that population grow by more than 25% in year D+1?

What you predict

target definition

Would be binary from the year-D+1 row's OBS_VALUE of the same origin: (OBS_VALUE[D+1] > 1.25 x OBS_VALUE[D]) with OBS_VALUE[D] >= 1000. Label source column: the TIME_PERIOD=D+1 OBS_VALUE, excluded from shipped features. Unit: persons. Frame: UNICEF MG_RFGS_CNTRY_ORIGIN, AGE=_T, country ISO3 REF_AREA only (155 aggregate REF_AREAs excluded).

When the prediction is made

the information cut-off

Would be the publication of the year-D value in UNICEF's MG series (annual data for year D appears during D+1; the SDMX rows carry no vintage field, so as-of reproducibility under UNHCR revisions is an open construction question).
