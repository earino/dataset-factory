The question

what a model is asked to predict

A Chilean public-sector tender (licitación) is published on Mercado Público. At the publication instant, given only publication-time context - the buying organism and its unit (CL-RUT identifier, region, municipality), tender title and description, item list with UNSPSC categories and quantities, estimated value and currency, procedure type (L1/LE/LP/LQ/LR public by UTM band, E2/CO/B2/H2/I2 private, LS specialised services), convocatoria type, payment/duration terms and the publication calendar position - will the process end deserted (desierta: no valid bids, Ley 19.886 art. 3 or 9) rather than adjudicated?

What you predict

target definition

Binary: the process's OCDS award status is 'unsuccessful' with statusDetails vocabulary '7-Desierta (o art. 3 ó 9 Ley 19.886)' (positive) vs 'active' / '8-Adjudicada' (negative). Measured on the first-100 samples of closed months: 17/98 = 17.3% desertion (2019-06), 14/98 = 14.3% (2023-06); cancelled/revoked ('15-Revocada', ~2-3%) are excluded from the scored population at construction with the exclusion recorded. Label source columns: award release status/statusDetails (+ tender final status as cross-check); excluded from shipped feature columns.

When the prediction is made

the information cut-off

The instant the tender is published on Mercado Público (tender publication date; OCDS release date). Everything decided or observed after publication is excluded from shipped features: the closing act, bids received and their counts, the evaluation, the adjudication/revocation decision and award documents, and contract fields. The OCDS award-stage status fields are the label source and are excluded from shipped columns. Construction note: a tender document fetched for a closed month carries the tender's final status/statusDetails (e.g. '5-Publicada' vs closed states) - construction must reconstruct publication-time state from publication-stage fields only.
