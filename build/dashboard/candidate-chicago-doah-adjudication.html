The question

what a model is asked to predict

For a building-code violation case docketed with the Chicago Department of Administrative Hearings, will the hearing officer ultimately find the respondent liable?

What you predict

target definition

Binary: case_disposition == 'Liable' versus 'Not Liable'. Cases disposed as Non-Suit, Default, Continuance, Adjudication Performance / Other, Dismissed Without Prejudice and Judgment Vacated are excluded from the binary task and reported separately as a multi-class follow-up.

When the prediction is made

the information cut-off

At docketing, before the hearing date. Permitted features are the violation, the property and the docket metadata. case_disposition, imposed_fine, admin_costs and last_modified_date are outcomes and excluded.
