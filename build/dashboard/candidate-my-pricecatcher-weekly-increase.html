my-pricecatcher-weekly-increase

ready · MY 09:05 UTC

The question

what a model is asked to predict

For an item-premise pair in Malaysia's official daily price-monitoring panel that was actively quoted in week W (at least 3 observations that week), will the pair's weekly modal price be HIGHER in week W+1 than in week W? The panel is the KPDN/DOSM PriceCatcher programme (2,158 premises, 343 items measured in the 2026-08/09 sample), so this is a week-ahead retail price-increase prediction at item x premise grain.

What you predict

target definition

Binary: modal(price of item i at premise p over all observations in ISO week W+1) > modal(same over week W). Modal price = most frequent observed price that week (ties broken by higher price, deterministic). Pairs with <3 observations in either week are excluded from that transition's rows (exclusion is a function of data through week W+1, applied identically everywhere; it affects ~3% of subsample pairs). The label source columns are the W+1 price observations themselves: no price observation with date > end of week W may ship as a feature column; the shipped target is 0/1.

When the prediction is made

the information cut-off

End of week W (Sunday 23:59:59 MYT = UTC+8, i.e. 15:59:59 UTC). Features may use only price observations with date <= end of week W, from files whose data_as_of is within week W or earlier. The publisher's storage channel is measured to carry rows dated the same day (2026-10-21 rows present in the 2026-10 file, file last-modified 2026-10-21 12:10 UTC), so no publication lag is incurred for the feature instant; the label (week W+1 modal price) is computable from files available by day 2 of week W+2 at the latest (current-month file refreshes daily; a completed month's file was last modified 12 days after month end, 2026-09 file -> 2026-10-12, so construction snapshots and hashes monthly files at freeze time - see construction notes).
