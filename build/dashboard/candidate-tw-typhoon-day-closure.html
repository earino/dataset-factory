tw-typhoon-day-closure

investigating · TW 11:50 UTC

The question

what a model is asked to predict

For a Taiwan county on a date D for which a typhoon land warning (陸上颱風警報) is active or being considered, will offices and schools be fully closed (全日停止辦公及上課) on D, predicted at the statutory announcement instant on the evening before D?

What you predict

target definition

Binary per (county, date): the county's status for date D is a full-day stop of both work and school, as published by the Directorate-General of Personnel Administration (DGPA). Label sources (excluded from shipped features): the DGPA 歷次天然災害停止上班上課訊息 archive (500 announcements, 2001-present, per-county status attachments) and, for the modern era, county-portal historical CSVs (e.g. data.taipei 臺北市歷次天然災害停止上班上課訊息, fields 民國年/月/日/天然災害名稱/停止上班上課情形).

When the prediction is made

the information cut-off

The announcement instant is fixed by regulation, quoted from the DGPA daily closure page (2026-10-22) and repeated verbatim in the announcement attachments: full-day or morning closures must be announced between 19:00 and 22:00 the previous evening (前一日晚間7時至10時前發布); same-morning reinforced closures are announced by 04:30 and broadcast by 05:00. Features may therefore use only data published before 19:00 Taipei time on D-1 (CWA warning bulletins issued through 17:10/20:10 are the boundary cases and must be pinned per-bulletin by issuance timestamp).
