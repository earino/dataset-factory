The question

what a model is asked to predict

The lead probed was the ITU DataHub (datahub.itu.int), the ITU's free dissemination channel for its ICT statistics: 'hundreds of ICT indicators' on 'about 200 economies' (connectivity, usage, affordability, price baskets, regulatory information), collected via annual questionnaires to governments. The task family it supports is a next-year country-level change forecast on an ICT indicator panel. Rejected on task shape and mirror grounds: annual country panels (persistence-dominated, positives clustered into a few global trend periods) already republished by the World Bank as a downloadable dataset.

What you predict

target definition

Would have been a binary change target on a country-year ICT indicator (e.g. will the share of individuals using the internet rise next year) - a level target is the measured persistence failure mode. Not reached: the mirror and licence findings close the lead first.

When the prediction is made

the information cut-off

Would have been the end of reference year Y (annual panels; the publisher updates on a questionnaire schedule - the site publishes a consolidated BDT data questionnaire schedule per year) with features from years <= Y. Workable in principle; not the failure.
