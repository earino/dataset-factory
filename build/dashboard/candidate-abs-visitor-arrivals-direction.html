abs-visitor-arrivals-direction

qualified · AU 11:50 UTC

The question

what a model is asked to predict

For a given country of residence, will short-term visitor arrivals to Australia in the first month not yet published (the month after the latest OAD release) fall short of the same calendar month one year earlier?

What you predict

target definition

Binary YoY direction: OBS_VALUE for (CAT_TRAVELLER=06 visitors arriving, COUNTRY_RESID=C, month M+1) < OBS_VALUE for (same series, month M-11). Label source columns: the target month's OBS_VALUE and the year-earlier OBS_VALUE; both excluded from shipped features.

When the prediction is made

the information cut-off

The publication instant of the ABS Overseas Arrivals and Departures (OAD) release for reference month M, which lands about six weeks after month end (max observed lag 43 days; releases observed 13/10/2026 for Aug 2026, 11/09/2026 for Jul 2026, 12/06/2026 for Apr 2026, all 11:30am local). Features are the published series values for months <= M only; the target month M+1 is unpublished at that instant (it is released ~43 days after its own month end).
