The question

what a model is asked to predict

Hypothetical task this source would support (parked, not taken to construction): for an English local authority, will its household waste recycling rate (recycled/reused/composted as a share of household waste) be at least 50% in financial year Y, given its published annual waste series for years before Y? The 50% threshold is the published Waste Framework Directive 2008/98/EC art.11(2)(a) target (50% household recycling by 2020) that England's LAs were tracked against, not an invented cutoff.

What you predict

target definition

Would be binary: Household - waste sent for recycling-composting-reuse (tonnes) / Household - total waste (tonnes) >= 0.50 for the LA in year Y, both columns from Defra's cumulative LA table. Label source columns: year Y's household tonnage columns (excluded from shipped features).

When the prediction is made

the information cut-off

Would be the publication date of the year Y-1 annual results (Defra publishes final year-Y figures roughly in Q4 of the following financial year; 2024/25 final figures were cut from WasteDataFlow in February 2026 and published in 2026). Features would come from published years <= Y-1; the label is computed from year Y's row, published later.
