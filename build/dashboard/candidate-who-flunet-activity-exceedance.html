published · CH · 2026-10-21 14:09 UTC

The question

what a model is asked to predict

For a WHO FluNet reporting country with a history of systematic weekly specimen reporting, will influenza test positivity in ISO week W+4 exceed a fixed activity threshold (proposed: 10% positive of specimens processed), given that country's weekly FluNet laboratory series (specimens processed, influenza A/B detections by subtype, non-influenza respiratory-virus detections) through ISO week W?

What you predict

target definition

Binary, computed from the source row of week W+4: influenza positivity = INF_ALL / (INF_ALL + INF_NEGATIVE) exceeds 0.10, with a minimum denominator of 50 specimens in the week. Unit: proportion of specimens testing positive for influenza. Reference frame: country x ISO week, SENTINEL and NONSENTINEL origin streams summed (the table's grain is country x ISO week x ORIGIN_SOURCE). Label source columns INF_ALL and INF_NEGATIVE are recorded in label_source_columns and must be absent from the shipped feature columns; the threshold and lead are to be pinned at construction against a named known-event fixture before any number is quoted.

When the prediction is made

the information cut-off

End of ISO week W (the Sunday that closes week W): every feature is derived from weekly laboratory counts published for weeks <= W; the target is week W+4's row, which is not yet observed. FluNet is updated weekly (measured: on 2026-10-20 the table already carries week-start 2026-10-19), so a monthly refresh of this task is honest.
