The question

what a model is asked to predict

For a Maryland collection system on a given day, will a sanitary sewer overflow be reported?

What you predict

target definition

Binary: at least one reported overflow for a facility on the target date. Event records collapse to facility-day units. Overflow type, cause and volume are outcomes and excluded.

When the prediction is made

the information cut-off

Start of the target day, using rainfall observations up to that moment, facility identity and season.
