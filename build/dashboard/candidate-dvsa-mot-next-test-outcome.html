dvsa-mot-next-test-outcome

investigating · GB 11:50 UTC

The question

what a model is asked to predict

For a vehicle with at least one completed MOT test, will its next MOT test end in an initial failure (test_result F, or PRS - pass only after rectification at the station) rather than a clean pass, given only what is known up to and including its previous completed test?

What you predict

target definition

Binary from the documented test_result field of the next test: positive if test_result is F or PRS - the DfT/DVSA documented 'initial failure' definition ('Initial failure rate = (Test Fail Results + Test PRS Results) / Total Tests', MOT Testing Data User Guide; the same definition drives the published DVSA/MOT/01 statistical tables). Negative if P. Abandoned/aborted codes (ABR, ABA - measured present at <0.6%) are excluded or handled at construction, to be decided and documented there.

When the prediction is made

the information cut-off

The completion instant of the vehicle's previous test (completed_date in the prior test's row). Features are the vehicle's static attributes (make, model, colour, fuel_type, cylinder_capacity, first_use_date, test class) plus its full prior-test history (test dates, intervals, test types including NT/RT, prior results, mileage and mileage deltas, prior defect-item categories from the companion failure-item files). The next test's own fields (test_result, its test_mileage, its completed_date, its defect items) are the label source and never ship as features.
