When the prediction is made

the information cut-off

Would be the publication of the Notice of Initiation in the Official Journal (the case's public start, also announced in TRON's RSS of newly initiated cases); the target is decided when the definitive regulation or termination decision publishes in the OJ, typically 13-14 months after initiation (policy.trade.ec.europa.eu states provisional measures within 7-8 months, definitive within 13-14). A genuine, well-documented timing story - one reason the source was opened.
