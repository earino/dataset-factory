The question

what a model is asked to predict

For a company on the live Companies House register at monthly snapshot M, will it exit the live register (be dissolved) within the following 12 months?

What you predict

target definition

Binary exit: the company is absent from the live register (or carries a dissolution status) at snapshot M+12. The label source is a LATER snapshot than the feature snapshot - which is exactly the blocker recorded in rejection_reason: Companies House publishes no bulk historical snapshots and no dissolved-companies bulk product, so the label cannot be observed from anything published today.

When the prediction is made

the information cut-off

Snapshot month M (the snapshot is compiled to the end of the previous month and published within 5 working days of month end). Features would be the register row at M: company category, SIC code(s), incorporation age, registered-address region, accounts/confirmation-statement filing state, mortgage counts, previous names - all present in the measured 55-column schema.
