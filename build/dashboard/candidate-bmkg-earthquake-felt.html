parked · ID 13:58 UTC

The question

what a model is asked to predict

For an earthquake detected by BMKG's national seismic network in Indonesia, will the event be reported as felt by the public (appear in the 'gempabumi dirasakan' product), given only instrumental parameters known at detection: magnitude, hypocenter depth, epicenter location and origin time?

What you predict

target definition

Binary felt / not-felt for a detected event: positive iff the event appears in BMKG's felt-earthquake product, negative iff it appears in the instrumental catalogue without a felt entry. The label source is BMKG's felt-report product (a documented public-report collection process), which is a later product than the features and never ships as a feature.

When the prediction is made

the information cut-off

The network's automatic processing instant for the event (minutes after origin): magnitude/depth/location are knowable then, and felt reports arrive only afterwards from the public reporting process.
