ready · EU 14:50 UTC

The question

what a model is asked to predict

For a stationary installation covered by the EU Emissions Trading System, will its verified emissions for compliance year t exceed the free allocation it holds for year t (a net-short year: the operator must buy allowances at auction/secondary market to surrender, or face the excess-emissions penalty), given its verified-emissions, free-allocation and surrender history through year t-1, its already-published year-t free allocation, its registry (country) and its name/identifier?

What you predict

target definition

Binary, computed from the source row of (installation, year t): VERIFIED_EMISSIONS(t) > ALLOCATION(t), where ALLOCATION is the free allocation recorded in the registry for year t and VERIFIED_EMISSIONS is the independently verified emissions figure. Unit: both are tonnes CO2-equivalent per installation-year (1 allowance = 1 tonne); the comparison is unit-free within the source's own frame (no external threshold or datum is involved). Frame restriction: installation-years where both VERIFIED_EMISSIONS and ALLOCATION are numeric ('n/a' rows - not-yet-operating, excluded or closed sites - are out of frame and their handling must be pinned at construction). Label source columns VERIFIED_EMISSIONS and ALLOCATION at year t are recorded in label_source_columns and must be absent from the shipped features; feature rows may use emissions/allocation/surrender values only for years <= t-1 plus year-t allocation.

When the prediction is made

the information cut-off

30 April of compliance year t, the surrender deadline that closes year t-1's compliance cycle: by then the installation's verified emissions and surrendered allowances for year t-1 are published in the Union Registry (verified emissions are due by 31 March and surrendered allowances by 30 April), and year t's free allocation is known in advance (fixed by the phase-4 national implementation measures decisions recorded in the registry - the extract already carries ALLOCATION for years up to 2030). Year t's verified emissions are not yet observed anywhere: they are reported by 31 March t+1. The extract refreshes daily (SNAPSHOT_DATE column, last-modified 2026-10-20), so a monthly refresh of this task is honest.
