The question

what a model is asked to predict

Given everything documented about an establishment and its own prior inspections before an inspection begins, which of the 61 violation codes will the inspector record for this visit?

What you predict

target definition

The set of violation codes recorded in the row's `violations` blob, parsed into codes 1-70 (multi-label, 61 observed codes; mean 3.08 per inspection). The label source column `violations` is excluded from every shipped file, together with `results` and the per-violation comment text.

When the prediction is made

the information cut-off

The moment the inspection is opened, before any observation: inspection_date is known when a canvass is scheduled or a complaint triggers a visit, and every intended feature is either a static facility attribute, the inspection's own type, or a field of an earlier inspection of the same licence.
